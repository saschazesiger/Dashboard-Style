Questions

What counts as a seat?

A person who can sign in. People who only own a device, or only get its reports, are free.

What happens after the trial?

We ask for a card on the last day. Without one, the workspace moves to Starter: nothing is deleted, devices past 25 stop syncing.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, at any time. Moving up is charged for the rest of the period; moving down applies from the next one.

Do you send invoices?

Every month or year, by email and on the billing page, with your tax ID once you add it.
