# P1: Foundation (1.0.0, ships together with P2)

Goal: the base every later phase builds on. P1 raises the browser floor to Safari 16.4, Chrome/Edge 111 and
Firefox 128 (D3, D28) and puts every stylesheet into `@layer ds.*` (D9). It removes reduced motion completely (D8),
rewrites the rules in AGENTS.md and the docs, and builds the new token system: the micro and long durations, the
spatial spring, the exit and value curves, the popup scales and the `--ds-time-scale` multiplier. A single curve
generator, `scripts/motion-curves.mjs`, produces every sampled curve. P1 also registers the motion hooks as
non-inheriting, rebuilds the keyframe catalogue on the individual `translate`/`scale` properties with movement-only
exits, and fixes the small bugs that this rework touches (`[hidden]`, backdrop click-through, tooltip fly-in,
`.ds-enter` on loops, hover movement).

Depends on P0: the Playwright harness, `test/browser/helpers/motion.js`, the fixtures (`motion.html`,
`controls.html`, `feedback.html`, `shell.html`, `module.html`, `reset.html`, `reset-layered.html`), the screenshot
and timeline baselines, the `?speed=` bridge, the helper self-tests for layered and indented CSS, and the
`test.fail` markers labelled "fixed in P1".

Ships: P1 is its own commit. It sets `package.json` to 1.0.0 and opens the `## 1.0.0` CHANGELOG section. P2 is
committed on top, and the two are pushed to `main` together (D31). The P1 commit must pass `npm run check` and
`npm run test:browser` in all three engines on its own.

Not in P1: the JS engine (`DS.motion` timing from tokens, interruption, FLIP, ripple; P2), the press-shape roles
`--ds-radius-action-pressed` / `--ds-radius-card-pressed` and every press, focus-snap or control change (P3), the
indicator (P4), the overlay choreography (P5), and loaders, skeleton and collapsible polish (P6).

- [ ] The spring (D1). `--ds-ease-spatial` is the step response of a damped spring with ζ = 0.75:
  `x(t) = 1 − e^(−ζωt)·(cos ω_d t + ζω/ω_d · sin ω_d t)`. It is normalised so that t = 1 is its settle time, the
  last moment |x − 1| exceeds 0.001. Expected shape:
  - peak 1.028 at about 59% of the duration;
  - x(0.2) ≈ 0.555, x(0.4) ≈ 0.959, x(0.5) ≈ 1.017, x(0.8) ≈ 1.012, x(1) = 1.

  Because the curve is normalised, one curve serves every size: small controls take `--ds-dur-medium`; menus,
  dialogs, FLIP and the indicator take `--ds-dur-slow`; sheets, the drawer and container transforms take
  `--ds-dur-long`. There are no `--ds-dur-spatial-*`, `--ds-dur-spring-*`, `--ds-ease-spring` or
  `--ds-ease-spatial-fast` tokens. The contract is the overshoot percentage, not ζ.
  [core-tokens-springs, controls-spatial-easing-token, repo-opp-spatial-springs]
