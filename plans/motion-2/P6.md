# P6: Feedback and data (1.4.0)

Goal: the feedback and data surfaces get their Material 3 Expressive motion (D10, D11, D25, D26, D29, D34). The
shape-morphing loader becomes the block loader, the spinner draws the growing and shrinking arc, progress gets the
2024 look (gap, stop dot, wave) and the two-segment indeterminate choreography, and skeletons run one calm diagonal
wave. Data glides as D7 asks: tables (hover layer, selection, expandable rows, FLIP for sort, filter and live data),
status chips, tiles, alerts and empty states. Clickable cards and the press morph on tiles arrive, and
`.ds-collapsible` gets asymmetric timing, a chevron and an unclipped focus ring.

Depends on:
- **P0**: the capability spike, which decides two things here: whether pseudo-element animations show up in
  `document.getAnimations()` (the loader and progress tests rely on it), and whether `translate` and
  `position: relative` work on `<tr>` (row FLIP and the row ripple). Also the layout baselines and the `test.fail`
  markers.
- **P1**: layers, the token system with `--ds-time-scale`, `scripts/motion-curves.mjs`, the keyframe-shape test, the
  loop exemptions and the animated-property allowlist with its `EXCEPTIONS` and `PENDING` maps.
- **P2**: `flip`, `morphHtml` (patch in place, `data-ds-key`, insert and remove hooks), `countUp`,
  `settleLoader(startedAt, { element })`, `crossfade`, and `DS.ripple` with `ripple.css`.
- **P3**: press timing, the focus snap and focus layer, the press roles `--ds-radius-action-pressed` and
  `--ds-radius-card-pressed`, the custom checkbox, `.ds-icon-swap` and the `'icon'` kind of `crossfade`.

Ships as **1.4.0**. D31 makes every 1.x release additive: no name is removed or renamed in this phase. D10 and D11
deliberately change the default look of loaders, progress and skeletons. Each of those changes gets its own line
under "Changed" in the changelog, saying what a project has to do (usually nothing; at most: use `--flat` or a hook).

  `/* generated: <name> */` markers, into `src/motion.css` (keyframe stops) and `src/tokens.css` (mask data URIs):
  - **Loader shapes**: the 7 M3 shapes in this order: soft burst, cookie-9, pentagon, pill, sunny, cookie-4, oval.
    Each is resampled to the same 64 vertices (start angle and winding normalised, so the morph never twists) and
    written as `polygon()` percentages with at most 2 decimals.
  - **Indeterminate progress**: the M3 two-segment choreography on a 1800ms cycle. Head 1 runs 0-750ms, tail 1
    333-1183ms, head 2 1000-1567ms, tail 2 1267-1800ms, each sampled from its M3 curve into stops with linear
    interpolation. The output is two keyframes: `ds-progress-head` animates `--ds-progress-head-1` and
    `--ds-progress-head-2`, and `ds-progress-tail` animates both tails. All values lie in 0%-100% and never
    decrease within a cycle.
  - **Spinner arc**: the M3 arc. The sweep `--ds-spinner-arc` goes 10deg → 270deg → 10deg, while the start
    `--ds-spinner-start` advances 270deg per cycle, so the tail chases the head. Sampled into `ds-spinner-arc`.
  - **Skeleton wave**: a symmetric sine in-out sweep that fills the first 60% of the cycle and then rests
    off-screen, sampled into `ds-skeleton-wave`.
  - **Wave masks**: an SVG sine stroke (4px stroke, ±3px amplitude, 40px wavelength) as `--ds-progress-wave`, and
    a closed wavy ring for the 40px determinate ring as `--ds-progress-ring-wave`.
  - Note: the 270deg advance of `--ds-spinner-start` per arc cycle only returns to its start (mod 360deg) after
    four cycles (4 x 270deg = 3 turns, the same trick as M3's per-cycle offset), so `ds-spinner-arc` holds four
    1333ms cycles and `--ds-loop-arc` is `5332ms` (one keyframe loop), not 1333ms. Spinner and status rules keep
    `var(--ds-loop-arc)` as planned.
  - Note: `ds-skeleton-wave` sweeps `--ds-skeleton-x` from `-150vw` to `50vw`, not from `-100vw` to `100vw`: with the
    band at the middle of the 200vw gradient, -100vw puts the band half on screen at the left edge (and, repeated,
    100vw is the same picture), so the loop would pop. -150vw / 50vw put the band half a viewport off screen at both
    ends, with or without `background-repeat`, and are one 200vw period apart.
  - Note: pill and oval are drawn directly (a 0.6 capsule and a 0.64 ellipse on the -45deg diagonal); the other five
    follow the MaterialShapes values as rounded polygons. Every shape is resampled at equal arc length and scaled so
    its farthest point lies on the circle of radius 50% (it turns inside its box). The ring mask has 7 waves of
