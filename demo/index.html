Calm dashboards, ready to theme.

A design system for admin consoles in the Material 3 / Google console style: plain CSS, small dependency-free modules, one theme file. Everything on this page is live: press it, tab through it, resize it. It loads a bundle of only the components it uses.

Actions

Every action is a pill. Press and hold one: a ripple grows from your pointer and the pill turns squarer, then springs back when you let go. Press Tab: the focus ring is solid from the first frame. Hover never moves anything.

Buttons

Busy, then done

The button never changes its width.

Icon buttons, toggles and tooltips

Rest on a button: its tooltip waits half a second, the next one comes at once. Copy link swaps its icon to a check that draws itself; the pin fills while it is on.

Split and menu buttons

One pill, two targets: the action, and a menu of its variants. The menu button shows what was chosen; its chevron turns while the menu is open.

    Live data

    A fake backend reports new figures every few seconds. Tiles count on from what they show, rows glide to their new place, status chips fade to their new colour. Point at the table: a live reorder waits until you are done (at most 2s). Every active filter shows as an input chip: press one to remove its filter.

    Overlays

    Dialogs, side sheets, menus, popovers and snackbars open from where you pressed and close back there. Click twice quickly: a closing overlay turns around from where it is. Escape closes the top layer only, and the focus goes back to the button that opened it.

    Dialog and side sheet

    The dialog grows from its opener on the spring and stays open while its delete runs (Escape waits); the sheet slides in and steps between records with a shared-axis transition.

    Menu and snackbars

    Menus work with the arrow keys and type-ahead. Snackbars stack up to three; an error or one with an action stays until closed.
