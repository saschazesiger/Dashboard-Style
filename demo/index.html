Calm dashboards, ready to theme.

A design system for admin dashboards in the Material 3 / Google console style: plain CSS, a small dependency-free script, one theme file. Everything on this page is live: press it, tab through it, break it.

Switch the top bar to Slow motion to watch every animation at a fifth of its speed. The pill under it glides on the same spring as everything else.

Press, hover, focus

Every action is a pill. Press and hold one: a ripple grows from your pointer and the pill turns squarer, then springs back when you let go. Press Tab: the focus ring is solid from the first frame and snaps into place. Hover never moves anything.

Buttons

The filter is a toggle (aria-pressed). The last button stays focusable, so its tooltip can explain itself.

Icon buttons and tooltips

Rest on a button: its tooltip waits half a second, the next one comes at once (warm-up). Copy link swaps its icon to a check that draws itself.

Busy, then done

The button never changes its width.

Choose

Checks draw themselves from their short arm, switch handles glide on the spring, chips open room for their check and the chips after them glide along. Nothing animates on load.

Live data

A fake backend reports new figures every few seconds. Tiles count on from what they show, rows glide to their new place, status chips fade to their new colour. Point at the table: a live reorder waits until you are done (at most 2s). Type in the search: the rows glide per keystroke.

Overlays

Dialogs, side sheets, menus and snackbars open from where you pressed and close back there. Click twice quickly: a closing overlay turns around from where it is. Escape closes the top layer only, focus goes back to the button that opened it.

Dialog and side sheet

The dialog grows from its opener on the spring; the sheet slides its full width in and steps between records with a shared-axis transition.

Menu and snackbars

Menus work with the arrow keys. Snackbars stack up to three; an error or one with an action stays until closed.

Feedback

Loaders appear only after 200ms, so fast answers show nothing. Progress glides and lands on the same frame as its figure. Alerts come and go while the ones around them glide.
